✍️ Grammar: Going to + Connectors

🎯 Topic: Future Plans with Going to and Connectors (Travel, Culture & Communication)
1. What is "going to"?

Going to is used to talk about future plans and intentions, especially for trips, culture, and experiences.

Subject + am/is/are + going to + verb
📌 Example:I am going to travel and explore new places.🔊
2. What are connectors?

Connectors are words used to link ideas and organize sentences. They show time, sequence, and cause and result.

3. Types of connectors

⏰ A. Time connectors

BEFORE AFTER WHILE
📌 I am going to check my phone BEFORE the trip.🔊
📌 I am going to read a travel magazine AFTER the flight.🔊
📌 I am going to use my tablet WHILE I am traveling.🔊

📋 B. Sequence connectors

FIRST THEN FINALLY
📌 FIRST, I am going to search information on my tablet.🔊
📌 THEN, I am going to send an email to book a tour.🔊
📌 FINALLY, I am going to write a postcard about my experience.🔊

⚡ C. Cause and result connectors

BECAUSE SO THEN
📌 I love culture, SO I am going to read a newspaper.🔊
📌 I am going to send a letter BECAUSE I want to know people.🔊
📌 I have my phone, THEN I am going to communicate with my family.🔊
